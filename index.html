How we work

Assessment through to handover

Every project is run through the same four stages, so you always know what's happening, what's next, and who's responsible for it.

01
Assessment
We survey the property, understand the brief and the constraints, and set out what's achievable.
02
Planning
Design, specification, costing and programme are agreed and fixed before a single tool is on site.
03
Build
Our trades deliver the works to programme, with a dedicated manager reporting on progress weekly.
04
Handover
Snagging, sign-off and documentation are completed before we hand back a finished space.

Why CONCRAFT

Built on Experience. Focused on Your Home.

CONCRAFT brings the experience and expertise of Craftex into the residential sector, combining commercial construction standards with a personal approach to creating exceptional homes.

From extensions and loft conversions to renovations, refurbishments and complete property transformations, we provide a seamless building service from design and planning through to completion.

Our aim is simple — to take the complexity out of your project, manage every detail professionally and deliver a finished space that is built to last.
